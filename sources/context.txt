**Frontier research on emergent spacetime dynamics** (where spacetime, gravity, and matter arise from more fundamental quantum, informational, topological, or relational structures rather than being postulated as fundamental) intersects with the topics you mentioned, though exact phrase matches are rare. These ideas often appear in quantum gravity approaches like holography, tensor networks, topological phases, entanglement-based emergence, matrix models, and information-theoretic frameworks.

Here are relevant papers and connections, grouped by your subjects. Many blend multiple themes.

### Phase-Bound Matter (or Phase Boundary/Transition Matter in Emergent Contexts)
This relates to matter or spacetime emerging via **phase transitions**, topological phases, or boundaries in condensed-matter-inspired quantum gravity or early-universe models.

- **"Emergent Dynamics of Spacetime and Matter from a Topological Phase"** (Giandomenico Palumbo, arXiv:2205.06143, 2022; published in EPL 2023). A metric-independent fermionic gauge theory in a topological phase (no local propagating degrees of freedom, de Sitter invariant) undergoes spontaneous symmetry breaking. This yields dynamical Dirac fermions (matter) and Einstein gravity with positive cosmological constant and torsion. The phase transition/breaking induces both.

- **"Emergent spacetime from spatial energy potentiality: a new theoretical framework for early universe cosmology"** (Farrukh A. Chishtie, arXiv:2502.18524, v5 2026). Spacetime emerges via a first-order **phase transition** from a 3D spatial energy-functional configuration (no time initially) when quantum kinetic terms exceed a threshold. This resolves the Big Bang singularity (finite curvature), implements Starobinsky inflation, predicts gravitational waves, and addresses Hubble tension. Explicit phase-boundary dynamics.

Other works link phase boundaries/topological defects to emergent gravity/matter (e.g., in analog gravity or model sets/tilings).

### Frequency-Modulated Processes
This appears in models where oscillations, resonances, or modulations (in time/frequency domains) drive emergence, time dilation, or dynamics. It is less mainstream in core quantum gravity but shows up in extended or phenomenological frameworks.

- Works treating time or gravity via frequency modulation/oscillations (e.g., "Time-Entropy Mapping; Mass-Gravity Duality" preprints link frequency modulation to spacetime geometry deformation).

- Space-time modulated systems or metasurfaces in wave propagation, with analogs to emergent dynamics (e.g., nonreciprocal effects, bandgaps from modulation).

- Speculative/phenomenological papers on "Higgs-Frequency Gravity" or eon frequency modulation for time dilation/gravity. These are frontier-adjacent but connect modulation to emergent phenomena.

Orthogonal Time Frequency Space (OTFS) modulation and related tech papers exist but are more applied (communications) than fundamental physics.

### Information-Bearing Structures
Strong overlap here: Many emergent spacetime models treat reality as arising from quantum information, entanglement, or relational data structures. Spacetime/geometry emerges from patterns of information or entanglement.

- Entanglement-based emergence: Papers like "Spacetime Emergence from Quantum Entanglement" or "Quantum entanglement dynamics of spacetime and matter" (e.g., ICQFT unifying via spacetime-matter entanglement).

- Holographic/information-theoretic views: Information-bearing surfaces/boundaries (e.g., horizons as information-bearing), discrete models with finite-information cells, or networks where geometry emerges from information relations.

- Broader: "Decoding Reality" frameworks (Vlatko Vedral et al.) and infodynamics where order emerges from information minimization.

### Mathematical Patterns Capable of Being Decoded
This ties into decoding hidden structures, symmetries, fractals, or codes underlying spacetime (e.g., via category theory, fractals, or quantum information decoding).

- Fractal/golden-ratio patterns in quantum networks or self-organizing geometries (e.g., "Deciphering the Universe as a Quantum Information Network" with Fibonacci/Lucas-based 3D fractal patterns).

- Category theory for decoding quantum gravity structures.

- Model sets/tilings or spectral dynamics where aperiodic mathematical patterns yield emergent spacetime/matter.

- General quantum information approaches emphasizing decodable patterns in entanglement or pregeometric structures.

**Key overarching resources**:
- Reviews on emergent spacetime scenarios (condensed matter analogs, lattice models, geometrogenesis as phase transitions).
- Tensor/matrix models, Regge calculus/discrete spacetime, and holographic projections where information patterns decode into geometry.

These topics are highly interdisciplinary and speculative at the frontier—often combining ideas from condensed matter, quantum info, and gravity. arXiv (hep-th, gr-qc, quant-ph) and sites like Quantum Gravity Research host many related preprints. Search terms like "emergent spacetime entanglement," "geometrogenesis," "topological phase gravity," or "holographic information emergence" yield more.
